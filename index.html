Atto XO / Early-stage platform

Research-first trading intelligence.

We are building a Rust-first platform for point-in-time market research, model evaluation, risk controls and explainable trade recommendations.

Design targets

The proposed pipeline runs on a ten-minute cadence, aims to publish up to five ranked suggestions within eight minutes, and makes dataset/model provenance and risk checks explicit. These are targets, not operational performance claims.

Research and paper-trading first.

This is not a live brokerage product, personalized investment advice, or a promise of returns. Markets involve risk, including loss of capital. Real-money execution is separately gated and not enabled by this page.
